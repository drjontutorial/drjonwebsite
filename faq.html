06

There is no single "correct" time to start, as every student has different goals and starting points.

For students aiming to improve their understanding and confidence, I recommend seeking support as soon as they begin to struggle with a topic. Addressing difficulties early is often more effective than trying to catch up shortly before examinations.

For students targeting Grade 9, A*, Oxbridge, TMUA, ESAT, STEP, SAT, 13+ or 16+ exams, I generally recommend starting preparation as early as possible. This allows sufficient time to build deep subject understanding, develop problem-solving skills, and address weaknesses without relying on last-minute revision.

Many of my students begin preparation one to two years before their final examinations, particularly when targeting the most competitive universities and courses.

Starting early allows students to build these skills gradually rather than relying on last-minute preparation.

A lesson can be cancelled or rearranged up to 24 hours before the scheduled lesson time. To cancel or rearrange a lesson, please contact me as early as possible by phone, WhatsApp or email.

If a lesson is cancelled within 24 hours of the scheduled lesson time, the lesson cannot normally be rearranged and the full lesson fee will apply.

However, I understand that unexpected situations can happen. Therefore, each student is offered two fee-waiver opportunities per academic year, from 1st September to 31st August, for lessons cancelled within 24 hours.
